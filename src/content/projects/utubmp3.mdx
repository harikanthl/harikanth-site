utubmp3 adds a **⬇ MP3** button next to Like and Share on YouTube. Click it, and a few seconds later the song is in your Downloads folder as a properly tagged MP3 with its cover art. Install the app, open it once, turn on the extension, and that's the whole setup. No Terminal, Homebrew, or Python.

## What it does

- **One-click MP3**: a button sits right in YouTube's action bar (it floats in the corner on Shorts). It turns into ⏳ while converting and ✅ **Saved** when done; click it to show the file in Finder.
- **Tags that are actually right**: every download is tagged with the title, singers, album or movie, composer, lyricist, and label, parsed from the video description. Hashtags, social links, and the pasted-in description are stripped out, and the file is renamed to `Title - Album.mp3`.
- **A tag editor in the toolbar**: the popup lists your recent MP3s with **Clean** and **Edit** buttons. Edit any tag, or clear a field to remove it. Audio and cover art are never re-encoded.
- **Keeps working when YouTube changes**: the downloader updates itself daily, and if a download fails it updates and retries once on the spot.
- **Runs on your Mac**: nothing leaves your machine except the download from YouTube. No account, no analytics, no servers.

## Install

1. Download the DMG, drag **utubmp3** to Applications, and open it once.
2. Click **Quit and Open Safari Settings…** and turn on the extension. Allow it on youtube.com when Safari asks.
3. Open any YouTube video and click **⬇ MP3**.

**Using Chrome?** Install and open the Mac app first (it runs the downloads), then download the Chrome extension, unzip it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, and click **Load unpacked**. Google's Web Store doesn't allow YouTube downloaders, so this is the only way to install it in Chrome.

## How it works

Browser extensions aren't allowed to run programs, so the app ships a small background helper: the same executable, started at login by `launchd`. The extension talks to it over a local connection, and the helper does the heavy lifting with `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg`.

```text
YouTube page → Safari / Chrome extension → helper (127.0.0.1) → yt-dlp + ffmpeg → ~/Downloads
```

- The helper only listens on `127.0.0.1`, refuses requests from web pages (and DNS-rebinding tricks), only accepts YouTube video links, and only touches `.mp3` files directly in Downloads.
- `ffmpeg` and the QuickJS runtime are bundled and signed; `yt-dlp` is downloaded on first run so it can update itself, since YouTube regularly breaks old versions.
- The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and runs outside the App Store because a sandboxed app can't start the helper.

## Why I built it

Online converter sites are full of pop-ups and hand back files with junk names and no tags. utubmp3 does the same job from a button on the video itself, runs entirely on the Mac, and hands back a file that's named and tagged the way a music app expects.

Only download content you have the rights to, such as your own uploads or openly licensed videos. Downloading from YouTube may be against its Terms of Service.
